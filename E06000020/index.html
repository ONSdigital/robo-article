Total population in Telford and Wrekin

In 2024, Telford and Wrekin was the 10th largest local authority in the West Midlands, with a population of almost 200,000. Since 2001, its population has increased by 23.6%.

Population density in Telford and Wrekin

In 2024, the population density in Telford and Wrekin was 675 people per square kilometre, which was more than the West Midlands (476), and more than the United Kingdom as a whole (285).

Median age in Telford and Wrekin

In 2024, the median age in Telford and Wrekin was 39 years, which was the same as the West Midlands (39 years), and younger than the United Kingdom as a whole (40 years).
